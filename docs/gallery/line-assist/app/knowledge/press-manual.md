# Hydraulic press 400 t: service manual (synthetic)

## Lockout before service
Stop the press at top dead center, lower the ram onto the safety blocks, switch off the main
isolator and the pump, apply a personal lock and tag, and bleed accumulator pressure to zero
before opening any hydraulic line.

## Alarm E-214 hydraulic oil overtemperature
The oil is above 65 C. Common causes are a failed oil-cooler fan, a blocked cooler core, low oil
level, or a relief valve stuck partly open. Check the cooler fan current first, then the oil level
sight glass, then the cooler core for dust.

## Slow ram on the down stroke
Hot, thin oil and internal leakage in the pump reduce pressure, so the ram slows. Compare pump
pressure at the gauge with the nominal 205 bar. If pressure recovers once the oil cools, fix the
cooling first.

## Oil cooler fan
The fan motor draws about 3.1 A. A reading near zero means a blown fuse F-21, a seized motor or a
broken wire at connector X-40. Spare fan motor: part 4410-221. Fuse: part 7702-016.
